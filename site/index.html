Most replies are routine, and they still take time.

People answer the same kinds of mail every day: is the date confirmed, did the document arrive, can we move the call. Quick-reply buttons only offer a few fixed phrases such as "Thank you". Out-of-office auto-responders send the same text to everyone. Neither can answer a specific question.

Smart Reply fills the gap between those and writing every message by hand. It reads the actual message and drafts a complete answer, and it leaves the final decision to you.

How it works

  1. Sign in and connect your mailbox

    You grant limited access through Google's own consent screen. The app never sees your password and you can revoke access at any time.

  2. Pick a message and a tone

    Your recent mail appears on the dashboard. Newsletters and no-reply senders are marked as not suitable, so you only see messages worth answering.

  3. Review the draft

    The draft opens next to the original email. Edit the text, choose another tone, or regenerate it.

  4. Approve and send

    Pressing send is the only action that sends mail. The reply is recorded in your history with its status.

What it does

Context-aware drafts
Replies are built from the latest message and a short summary of the thread, so old quoted text is not mistaken for the new question.
Tone control
Choose formal, friendly or concise. Add a one-line instruction such as "decline politely and suggest next week" and regenerate.
You stay in control
Every draft is editable and nothing is sent without your approval. This is the rule the whole project is designed around.
Inbox filtering
Automated mail, newsletters and no-reply addresses are detected and excluded from reply generation.
Reply history
Generated, sent and discarded replies are listed with their date and status, so you can find any reply later.
Usage dashboard
See how many messages were processed, how many replies were generated and sent, and the time saved.
Safe sign-in
Sign-in goes through Google OAuth 2.0 with limited permissions: read the inbox and send mail. Nothing else is requested.
No invented facts
Drafts only reuse what the original email says. The engine never adds a date, price or promise the sender did not mention.

How it's built

Technology

InterfaceHTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Thymeleaf
ServerJava 17, Spring Boot, Spring MVC
SecuritySpring Security with Google OAuth 2.0, CSRF protection
MailGmail REST API, read inbox and send
Build and testMaven, JUnit, Postman, Git
HostingDocker on Render

What was tested

  • Registration and sign-in with valid and invalid input
  • Private pages refuse requests that are not signed in
  • No-reply and bulk mail is marked not suitable for a reply
  • Regenerating with an instruction produces a new draft
  • An approved reply is sent and saved with the status Sent
  • A friendly error appears when the mail service is unreachable
